RosterBuddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 August 2026
Developer: DonkeyWorks
App: RosterBuddy
Contact: donkeyworks26@gmail.com

Overview

RosterBuddy is a roster, pay-estimate, recovery, and shift-planning app designed for shift workers.

RosterBuddy is designed as a local-first app. This means your roster, pay settings, notes, recovery information, and app preferences are stored on your device unless you choose to export, back up, copy, or share that information yourself.

DonkeyWorks does not operate a RosterBuddy cloud account system and does not collect your roster or pay data through the app.

Information stored by the app

RosterBuddy may store the following information locally on your device:

  • roster entries, shift times, shift types, and selected dates;
  • pay-rate settings, allowance settings, tax-estimate settings, and pay-cycle settings;
  • public-holiday or manual modifier settings;
  • recovery, sleep, fatigue, or planning notes entered by you;
  • app preferences, display settings, and backup/export state.

This information is used only to provide the app's roster, pay-estimate, recovery, export, and planning features.

Information collected by DonkeyWorks

DonkeyWorks does not collect personal information directly through RosterBuddy.

RosterBuddy does not currently include:

  • DonkeyWorks user accounts;
  • DonkeyWorks cloud sync;
  • DonkeyWorks analytics tracking;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • third-party advertising SDKs;
  • sale of personal information.

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Backups, exports, and sharing

RosterBuddy may allow you to export, back up, copy, or share your roster and app data.

When you use these features, you choose where that information goes. For example, you may save a backup file to your device, copy text to the clipboard, share a roster through another app, or export a calendar file.

DonkeyWorks is not responsible for the privacy or security practices of other apps, services, people, or storage locations you choose to share exported RosterBuddy information with.

Device storage and deletion

RosterBuddy stores app data on your device using app-local storage and related Android storage features.

You can remove locally stored RosterBuddy data by using in-app reset/delete features where available, clearing the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Uninstalling or clearing app storage may permanently delete your local RosterBuddy data unless you have made your own backup first.

Pay, tax, and roster estimates

RosterBuddy may provide pay, allowance, tax, recovery, and roster calculations. These are estimates only.

RosterBuddy is not payroll, legal, financial, workplace-relations, or tax advice. You should check official payslips, workplace agreements, employer information, and relevant government guidance before relying on any calculation.

Children

RosterBuddy is intended for workers and is not directed at children.

Security

DonkeyWorks aims to design RosterBuddy so that sensitive roster and pay information remains under your control on your own device. However, no app, device, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your device, backups, and exported files secure.

Changes to this policy

DonkeyWorks may update this Privacy Policy when RosterBuddy changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change.

The updated policy will be made available through the app listing or support page.

Contact
