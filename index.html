RosterBuddy

Roster planning, pay estimates, and recovery tools for shift workers.

RosterBuddy helps shift workers plan rosters, estimate pay, and keep track of work patterns without turning life into a spreadsheet.

Built by DonkeyWorks, RosterBuddy is designed to be local-first: roster, pay settings, notes, and preferences stay on your device unless you choose to export, back up, copy, or share them.

Important disclaimer

RosterBuddy provides estimates only. Pay, tax, allowance, roster, and recovery calculations may not match your employer's payroll system, workplace agreement, tax situation, or final payslip.

RosterBuddy is not payroll, legal, workplace-relations, financial, tax, or medical advice.
