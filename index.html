Night loading Loading applies to paid hours worked between these times, including after midnight. Leave "Until" blank to load from the start time until midnight only.

Recovery toolkit

Sleep anchor Aim for one protected main sleep block after nights, plus a short pre-shift nap when the next shift is close. Safe Work Australia fatigue guidance
Light and caffeine timing Use bright light early in a night shift, then cut caffeine several hours before sleep and keep the trip home dim where practical. Healthdirect work-life balance
Active recovery On heavy fatigue days, pick gentle movement, hydration, regular food, and a low-demand reset instead of hard training. Comcare fatigue guidance
Burnout check Watch for emotional exhaustion, cynicism, poor concentration, irritability, and feeling ineffective. If it persists, escalate early. Healthdirect work-related stress
Ask for backup If fatigue affects driving, medication handling, patient care, machinery, or judgement, treat it as a safety issue. Speak to a trusted colleague, manager, or your EAP early. Healthdirect wellbeing at work

      Privacy

      Local-first privacy

      Your roster stays on this device. RosterBuddy does not provide automatic cloud sync until you choose it. You consent to optional cloud sync in Settings. The app uses private WebView storage for roster data and private Android storage as a widget mirror.
      Backups are files you controlExported JSON backups can contain rosters, notes, reminders, pay rules, and settings. Store them somewhere safe.
      Widgets mirror roster stateAndroid widgets need a small local copy so they can display shifts outside the WebView.
      No cloud account yetChanging phones or reinstalling requires an exported backup JSON.
