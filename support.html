About RosterBuddy

RosterBuddy helps shift workers plan rosters, estimate pay, track shift details, and manage recovery around changing work patterns.

The app is designed to be local-first. Your roster and pay settings are stored on your device unless you choose to export, back up, copy, or share them.

Getting help

For help with RosterBuddy, contact DonkeyWorks at donkeyworks26@gmail.com.

When requesting support, please include:

  • your device model;
  • Android version;
  • RosterBuddy app version;
  • a short description of the issue;
  • screenshots if useful;
  • whether the issue happened after an app update, backup restore, or settings change.

Please do not send sensitive pay, roster, medical, or workplace information unless it is necessary for the support request. If you do send screenshots, check them first and hide anything private.

Data and backups

RosterBuddy stores app data locally on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear app storage, your RosterBuddy data may be deleted.

Before uninstalling, changing devices, or clearing storage, use RosterBuddy's export or backup features if available.

Exported files are your responsibility to store safely.

Pay and tax calculations

RosterBuddy calculations are estimates only. They may not match your employer's payroll system, workplace agreement, tax situation, allowances, deductions, or final payslip.

Always check official payslips, employer information, workplace agreements, and relevant government guidance before relying on any pay or tax estimate.

Feature requests and feedback

DonkeyWorks welcomes practical feedback, bug reports, and feature requests.

Useful feedback includes:

  • what you expected to happen;
  • what actually happened;
  • the steps to reproduce the problem;
  • whether it happens every time or occasionally;
  • whether the issue affects roster entry, pay estimates, recovery planning, backup/export, or display.
